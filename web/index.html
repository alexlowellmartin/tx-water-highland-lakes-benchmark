Highland Lakes from space

Surface water of the Texas Highland Lakes mapped from Sentinel-2 (optical) and Sentinel-1 (radar, sees through cloud) with a U-Net fine-tuned from TorchGeo pretrained weights, compared with the official lake level from the Texas Water Development Board. Each lake is mapped by a model that never saw that lake during training.

Transfer test: small reservoirs elsewhere in Texas that no model saw in training, mapped as if they had no gauge (their TWDB record is used only for scoring).
