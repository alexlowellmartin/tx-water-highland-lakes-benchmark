How to label
  • Judge only the single 10 m pixel inside the magenta corner marks, as it was on that date.
  • Water: open water, including muddy, green or glinting water and water under thin haze.
  • Not water: everything else, including exposed lakebed, wet mud, docks, bridges, boats and emergent vegetation. Judge what covers most of the 10 m pixel: a cable over water is still water.
  • Unsure: about half and half, or you can't tell. These are dropped from scoring, so commit when you can lean one way, and use it honestly rather than guessing.
  • When you finish, use Review labels to skim everything you called water, then not water, and fix any slips.
  • The false-colour view is usually decisive: water absorbs short-wave infrared and looks very dark there.
  • Progress saves in this browser automatically. Export when done (or any time) and save it in labels/ under your own name, e.g. hand_points_yourname.csv (hand_points_human.csv is the author's), then run make human-score LABELS=labels/hand_points_yourname.csv.
  • Shoreline points come first. They matter most, because that is where methods disagree.
