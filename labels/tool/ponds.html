How to label
  • Judge the area inside the cyan line, as it was on the photo date.
  • Water: nearly all of it (roughly 90 %+) is open water, including muddy, green, algae-covered or sun-glinting water.
  • Partly water: a real pond, but a sizeable part of the outlined area is not water (mud, vegetation, shadow, a dam, a road).
  • Not water: little or none of it is water: a tree shadow, a dark field, a roof, a parking lot, a dry tank bed.
  • Unsure: you honestly can't tell. Dropped from the numbers.
  • Outline misses water (separate checkbox, optional): tick it if obvious water of the same pond lies outside the line. This is what drives area under-counts, which the first question can't see.
  • Colour infrared is usually decisive: water is dark or blue-green there, and anything red is growing plants.
  • Sizes and the AI's earlier answers are hidden on purpose. Progress saves in this browser. Export and save the file as labels/ponds_naip_audit_human.csv.
